DGCA CPL Essential formulas for fast cockpit calculation drills:

CONCEPT STANDARD FORMULA COCKPIT RULE OF THUMB
Departure (Distance E/W) Dep = d'long (min) × cos(Mean Lat) 1° d'long at 60° Lat = 30 NM
Convergency Convergency = d'long × sin(Mean Lat) At Equator: 0 | At Poles: d'long
Conversion Angle CA = ½ × Convergency Difference between GC & Rhumb Line
1 in 60 Rule Track Error (°) = (Off Track Distance / Dist Flown) × 60 1 NM off at 60 NM = 1° drift
Rate of Descent (3° GP) ROD (ft/min) ≈ Groundspeed × 5 120 kt × 5 = 600 ft/min
Top of Descent (TOD) Distance (NM) = Altitude to lose (ft) / 300 30,000 ft / 300 = 100 NM
Radius of Turn R (NM) = TAS² / (68,000 × tan θ) Rate 1 turn = 3°/sec
PNR (Point of Safe Return) Time to PNR = (E × H) / (O + H) E=Safe Endur, O=GS Out, H=GS Home
Critical Point (CP) Dist to CP = (D × H) / (O + H) D=Total dist, O=GS Out, H=GS Home
